I'm currently an AI Safety Research Associate at [AI Singapore](https://aisingapore.org/), NTU, and an incoming CS PhD student at the [National University of Singapore](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/) (NUS).

My research spans **AI safety**, **AI alignment**, and **cooperative AI**: making models robust under
adversarial pressure, aligning them with human intent beyond the training distribution, and studying
how AI systems and people can coordinate without one party's goals overriding everyone else's. That
last problem is why I believe the future of AI will be **pluralistic** — human values are plural and
often incompatible, so alignment should treat disagreement as a design constraint rather than as
noise to be averaged away.

I hold a Master of Computing in Artificial Intelligence from NUS (2026), a Bachelor of Computing from [BINUS University](https://binus.ac.id/) (2026), and a Bachelor of Science from [Universitas Gadjah Mada](https://ugm.ac.id/) (2025).
